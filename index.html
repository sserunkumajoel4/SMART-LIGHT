About

SMART LIGHT TECHNOLOGY

Smart Light Technology is an intelligent lighting prototype developed to demonstrate the integration of software, embedded electronics and automated control into a practical lighting system.

The system allows users to control two independent lights, manage brightness where supported, create lighting schedules and monitor system information from a smartphone.
